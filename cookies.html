Cookies

This website only uses analytics cookies, and only if you accept them.

What they are for

Google Analytics tells me, anonymously, which pages, photographs and films people look at most, and where visitors come from. It helps me decide what to show and which prints to offer. Your IP address is anonymised and I do not use this information for advertising.

Which cookies

_ga and _ga_* (Google Analytics, by Google Ireland Ltd.): they tell visits apart. They last up to 2 years. If you decline, they are never created.

The site also remembers in your browser whether you accepted or declined, and whether you chose dark mode. This is not shared with anyone.

Change your choice

You can accept or decline again at any time.
